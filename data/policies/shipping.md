# Shipping and delivery

## Where we deliver
We deliver to all governorates in Egypt. We do not offer international shipping yet, so we cannot deliver to addresses outside Egypt (for example Saudi Arabia, the UAE or Europe).

## Delivery times
Orders to Cairo and Giza arrive in 1 to 2 business days. Orders to other governorates arrive in 3 to 5 business days.

## Shipping fees
Shipping is free on orders above 1,500 EGP. Orders below 1,500 EGP have a flat shipping fee of 75 EGP.

## Tracking your order
When your order ships you receive a tracking number by SMS and email. You can also ask our assistant for your order status at any time.

## Late deliveries
If your order is more than 3 business days late, contact us and we will investigate with the courier and give you an update within 24 hours.
