# Payments and account

## Payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards, cash on delivery, and mobile wallets. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 10,000 EGP.

## Charged twice
If you see two charges for one order, the second one is usually a temporary authorization that disappears within 5 business days. If it is still there after 5 days, contact us with your order number.

## Customer care hours
Our human customer care team is available Sunday to Thursday, 9 AM to 9 PM Cairo time. The AI assistant is available 24/7 and can hand your conversation to a human agent at any time.
