# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging, with all accessories. The 30 days are counted from the delivery date shown on your order.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 business days after the refund is approved. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer or mobile wallet.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, report it within 7 days of delivery. We arrange a free pickup and you get a full refund or a replacement, your choice.

## Items that cannot be returned
Opened software, gift cards, and personal-care items (such as earphones once the seal is broken) cannot be returned unless they are faulty.

## Refund approval
Every refund is reviewed by a member of our customer care team before it is issued. This usually takes less than one business day.
